// COMMON PITFALL

Malaysian Malay is not Indonesian

The two languages share roots, but everyday game vocabulary is often different. Shipping an Indonesian build to Malaysia reads as foreign at once and players notice in the first menu.

Bonus: Malay has no grammatical gender, so even the pronoun "dia" means both he and she. That makes player-gender choices easier to localize when someone who knows the language plans for it from the start.
