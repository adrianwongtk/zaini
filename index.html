Linguistic Expertise

Malaysian Malay is not Indonesian

Although the two languages share common roots, much everyday and technical vocabulary differs. Content localized for Indonesia reads as foreign to Malaysian users, often from the very first screen.

A practical advantage: Malay has no grammatical gender, and the pronoun "dia" refers to both he and she. With early planning, this makes gender-neutral content considerably simpler to localize.
